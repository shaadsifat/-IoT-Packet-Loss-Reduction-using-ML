Pre-send columns are generated the instant before each packet leaves the router; post-send columns (state, dbytes, sloss, dloss, packet_lost) reflect what actually happened. "Action" is the Stage-1 rule's decision for that packet's Spkts/sttl zone — always NONE under no-protection. "model_risk_pct" is only populated when the Random Forest actually refined that packet's risk (zones MODERATE and up, under Math + ML).
